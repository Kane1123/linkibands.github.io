    Most deaf children have hearing parents, and many families are still finding a way to talk. linki is not a language and does not replace one. It is one simple signal between a parent and a child, for everyday moments.

    Five signals, each one different

    Each signal has its own buzz rhythm and its own screen, so it can be told apart by feel and by sight. Whether children find them easy to learn is what the pilot will test.

    Nothing between the two bands

    A direct link

    The bands talk to each other by radio. No phone, no app, no router.

    Nothing stored online

    No account and no cloud. No data about your child goes to a server.

    Few words, on purpose

    A small, fixed set of plain words: LOOK, DINNER, WAIT, COME NOW, STOP.

    Short range

    Made for the same home, school or park. We will publish the tested range after the pilot.
